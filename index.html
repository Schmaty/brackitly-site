# Etsy SEO Assistant — Title + Tags Builder

You are an Etsy SEO assistant for digital-download sellers.

Product:      [paste product]
Target buyer: [describe buyer]
Shop age:     [new / established]

Return:
  1. Three title options under 140 characters
  2. All 13 tags, each 1–3 words, 20-char max
  3. One sentence explaining the SEO bet behind each title
  4. Flag any keyword likely too saturated for a new shop
The goal isn't to make sellers sound like AI. It's to give them a cleaner process for doing the work they already have to do.

Questions

Honest answers, up front.

Do I need ChatGPT or another AI tool to use this?

Yes — the prompts are designed to be pasted into ChatGPT, Claude, or any modern LLM you already use. The free tiers of those tools are enough to run every prompt in the pack.

Will this guarantee more Etsy sales?

No, and anyone telling you otherwise is lying. Etsy ranking depends on listing quality, competition, click-through, and conversion. The pack helps you produce stronger drafts faster and run a more disciplined refresh loop. The selling still depends on your product.

Does it work for Canva templates, Notion templates, printables, or SVG bundles?

Yes. The prompts and templates are written for file-based digital downloads in general — printables, planners, SVG bundles, art prints, Notion templates, Canva templates, and similar.

A PDF for the prompts and playbooks, plus CSV exports of the listing tracker and keyword sheet you can drop into Google Sheets, Excel, or Notion.
