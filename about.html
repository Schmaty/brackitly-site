About

Small, useful tools for people who sell digital products.

Brackitly is a small product brand. The focus is narrow: make the repetitive parts of running a digital shop easier to do, easier to track, and easier to improve.

What Brackitly will not do

No fake reviews. No fake scarcity. No pretending prompts guarantee search rankings. No posting where communities have asked sellers not to promote.

What Brackitly will do

Ship practical templates, keep copy plain, update products when platform rules change, and build from real seller workflows instead of generic AI hype.
