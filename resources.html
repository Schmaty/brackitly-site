Simple workflows for cleaner Etsy listings.

A few public notes from the Brackitly playbook. These double as useful content for launch posts once the social accounts are live.

Workflow

The 30-minute stale listing refresh

Pick one underperforming listing. Rebuild the title, tags, first paragraph, alt text, and mockup brief. Track before/after performance for 30 days.

Checklist

Before you publish a digital download

Confirm file type, dimensions, editability, usage rights, refund language, and where buyers find their download after purchase.

Research

How to use competitor titles without copying

Look for repeated buyer phrases and missing modifiers. Keep the search intent; change the angle, audience, style, or use case.
