About

Small, useful tools for people who sell digital products.

Brackitly is a small product brand. The focus is narrow on purpose: make the repetitive parts of running a digital shop easier to do, easier to track, and easier to improve — without the breathless AI-marketing energy.

What Brackitly won't do

No fake anything.

No fake reviews. No fake scarcity. No pretending prompts guarantee search rankings. No posting where communities have asked sellers not to promote.

What Brackitly will do

Ship practical templates.

Keep copy plain, update products when platform rules change, and build from real seller workflows instead of generic AI hype. Refunds without an interrogation.

Operating principles

Useful first. Clear over clever.

01

One pack at a time

We ship one product, polish it, learn from it, and only then build the next. No "30 packs in your dashboard" sprawl.

02

Honest copy

If a tool can save you 20 minutes per listing, we say 20 minutes — not "10× your shop overnight."

03

Updates included

Etsy changes its rules. AI tools change their interfaces. Buyers get one updated pack, not "v2, $29 upgrade."
